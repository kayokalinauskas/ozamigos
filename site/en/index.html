Voice that stays out of your game's way

Join the room and you're already in the conversation. Your mic only opens when you speak, and the background noise stays in your room.

  • RNNoise noise suppression
  • 60 to 90 ms on the same network
  • No other app needed

  • Only your voice gets through

    The RNNoise neural network runs on your PC and removes keyboard, mouse and fan noise before your voice goes out.

  • Automatic sensitivity

    Your mic opens when it recognizes a voice above the room noise. Prefer to tune it? Drag the threshold over the level meter.

  • Every friend at the right volume

    Mute and deafen in one click, each friend's volume up to 200%, "mute for me", and your choice of microphone and audio output.

  • Fast and light

    Voice has its own connection, separate from the video, and uses 64 kbps per friend, almost nothing while you're quiet.

How it works

  1. Install the app

    Download the installer and open it. From then on, it updates itself.

  2. Join with the group password

    Pick the name your friends know you by and use the password from whoever invited you.

  3. Call your friends and play

    Copy the room link and send it to the group. Whoever joins is already in the conversation; to show your game, click Transmitir (Stream).

Privacy

Your data stays with you. Neither this site nor the app uses cookies or analytics.

  • No sign-up
  • No ads
  • No tracking

  • On your computer

    Your name, the group password and your preferences are stored only in your browser or in the app. Noise suppression runs here too: the sound of your room never leaves your PC.

  • On the server

    It checks the password and introduces the people in the room. Rooms live only in memory and disappear when everyone leaves; the technical log has no name and no password.

  • Between PCs

    Voice, video and audio go straight from one PC to the other, always encrypted. As with any direct connection, people in the room can see each other's IP: use it with people you know.

Like any website, GitHub, Render and the Google server that helps PCs find each other (STUN) receive the IP address of whoever connects. The code is open: check it on GitHub.

FAQ

Didn't find your answer? Ask on GitHub.

Is the app in English?

Not yet: the app interface is in Brazilian Portuguese. It's simple to use, and the main buttons are Entrar na sala (join the room), Transmitir (stream) and Assistir (watch). The microphone, headphones and gear icons work just like in any voice chat.

Windows said it protected my PC. Is it safe?

The warning appears because the installer has no paid digital signature. Click More info and then Run anyway. The code is open, and every installer is built by GitHub from it, with a public history of every version.

Do I need a password?

Yes, the group password. Ask whoever invited you: it keeps strangers out of the rooms.

Is it free?

Yes. Ozamigos is free and open source, under the MIT license.

Can I use it without installing?

Yes. The portable version opens with a double click and is on the downloads page. Or use it in the browser (Chrome or Edge), voice included. Only the game audio goes without the separation the app does.

Do I need another app to talk?

No. Voice is built into Ozamigos: whoever joins the room is already in the conversation, with noise suppression, mute, per-friend volume and your choice of microphone and audio output.

Is there voice delay?

Very little. Voice goes straight from one PC to the other, on a connection of its own. On our test bench, on the same network, it arrives in 60 to 90 ms; over the internet, add the time packets take between your homes.

Does it work on Mac, Linux or phones?

The app is Windows 10 and 11 only. On other systems, use it in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Do my voice or my video go through a server?

No. The server only introduces the people in the room and checks the password. Your voice goes straight to each friend, and video and game audio go from the streamer only to those who clicked Assistir (watch), over WebRTC.

Why does the browser version sometimes take a while to open?

The server runs on a free plan that “naps” when nobody is using it. The first visit after a while can take up to a minute; after that, it opens right away.
